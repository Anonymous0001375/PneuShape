PneuShape: A Soft Pneumatic Gripper with Embedded Distributed Capacitive Shape Sensing for Closed-Loop Control

Anonymous Authors

Manuscript under review · anonymized for double-blind review

About this project

PneuShape is a soft pneumatic gripper with integrated proprioceptive shape sensing. Each finger carries a nine-channel differential capacitive sensor beneath a 3D-printed TPU core, and the whole assembly is molded in Ecoflex 00-30 silicone. A mold-supervised neural network maps the nine differential-capacitance measurements to a 27-point curve that describes the finger's shape in real time, so the gripper can perceive its own deformation without any external vision.

Using these reconstructed finger curves, we estimate contact, lateral and axial grasp offsets, and the cross-sectional shape of the grasped object. In our experiments the gripper reconstructs the cross-sections of six column-shaped objects with a mean RMSE of 5.8 mm, and it grasps objects placed at unknown off-center positions with a success rate of 95%.

Fabrication, PCB and firmware files

The sensor and gripper fabrication files (molds, TPU liners, and the capacitive sensor layers), together with the PCB design files and embedded firmware, will be released in this repository upon acceptance.
